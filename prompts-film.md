# Wicker as a lost 1973 film: Krea 2 stills and H3 video prompts

These prompts imagine the game's common moments as scenes from a lost British folk horror film shot in 1973 on Eastman colour negative. Each scenario has two prompts, and every prompt is complete in itself so it can be pasted straight in:

- **Krea 2 still:** a single frame from the film.
- **H3 video:** a fifteen-second sequence. Every one opens with your tested H3 style passage, unchanged, and then describes the action shot by shot.

The figures are described to match the game's models as they look for the Ochre tribe, the same descriptions used in `prompts.md`, but rendered as practical costume and props on real locations rather than as illustrations. The rival tribe in each scene is Blood (red), so the two sides stay easy to tell apart on screen.

**Before you start:**

- **Aspect ratio.** British features of the period were mostly shot flat at 1.85:1 or 1.66:1. Generate stills at 1.85:1 (for example 1850 × 1000) and video at 16:9, cropping to 1.85:1 afterwards if you want the letterbox.
- **Tribe colours.** These match the game: Ochre `#d39b3c`, Blood `#b8392f`, Forest `#33c4a0`, Ash `#d8d2c5`. On Eastman stock these will drift, so the prompts describe them as dyed cloth (a mustard-gold smock, a madder-red tunic) rather than giving hex codes. To change a tribe, swap the colour words.
- **Faces.** The models have no fine faces, so the prompts keep people at middle distance or turned away, which also suits the period's look. If a generator insists on close faces, add "faces in shadow or turned from camera".
